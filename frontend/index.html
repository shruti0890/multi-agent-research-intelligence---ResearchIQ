The Literature Dilemma

Research doesn't fail because there aren't enough papers.
It fails because the important connections are easy to miss.

01 // VOLUME

Too much literature

Relevant work is scattered across disconnected repositories, ambiguous terminology, and siloed academic disciplines.

02 // VERIFICATION

Gaps are difficult to prove

A missing connection or methodological omission across 10+ papers is significantly harder to establish than an isolated existing claim.

03 // PRIOR ART

Novelty has another lens

A method can appear completely novel in the academic literature while identical technical claims already exist in patent registries.

Find the Space Between What Exists and What's Next.

Research gaps emerge at the exact intersection where multiple independent research limitations converge.

Paper A: Hanabi Action Space Augmentation
Limitation: Fails under high-latency asynchronous networks
Paper B: Transformer MARL Decision Ordering
Limitation: Computation explodes beyond 8 agents ($O(N^2)$)
Paper C: CMA-ES Hybrid Strategy Evolution
Limitation: Requires full environment observability
⚠ CONVERGED CORE RESEARCH GAP

Non-Stationarity & Communication Bottlenecks Under Scaling

All three independent papers encounter severe degradation when scaling beyond localized, synchronous coordination.

Decentralized Meta-Learning Protocols for Zero-Shot Synchronization
